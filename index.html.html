Who Can Apply

Eligibility Requirements

NyotaFund Loans is open to all Kenyan citizens who meet the basic requirements below.

  • 🪪
    National ID / Passport

    You must be a Kenyan citizen aged 18 years and above with a valid National ID or passport.

  • 📱
    Active M-Pesa Account

    A registered Safaricom M-Pesa line used for at least 6 months, with regular transaction history.

  • 💼
    Source of Income

    Proof of employment, business income, or regular remittances over the last 3 months.

  • 📍
    Kenya Resident

    Must be a resident in Kenya with a verifiable physical address or utility bill.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does approval take? ▼
Emergency loans are processed within 2 hours. Personal and business loans are reviewed and approved within 24 hours of submitting complete documents.
What documents do I need? ▼
You'll need a copy of your National ID, your M-Pesa or bank statement for the last 3 months, and for business loans, a brief business plan or proof of trading.
How is the money sent to me? ▼
All disbursements are made directly to your registered M-Pesa number or bank account. We do not use third-party agents or middlemen.
Is NyotaProjectFund regulated? ▼
Yes. NyotaProjectFund operates in compliance with the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) digital lending regulations and the Data Protection Act 2019.
Can I repay early or top up my loan? ▼
Yes! Early repayment is allowed with no penalty. Clients with a good repayment history are also eligible for loan top-ups and higher limits on future applications.
What happens if I miss a repayment? ▼
We encourage clients to contact us before missing a payment. We offer grace periods and restructuring options. Persistent default may be reported to CRB agencies in line with Kenyan law.
